## Description
Stop guessing where to meet. MidPoint Meet helps you find convenient meeting locations between two people, whether you're meeting friends, coordinating a custody exchange or arranging a marketplace pickup.

Explore restaurants, coffee shops, gas stations, rest areas, points of interest and public meeting locations. Share your proposed destination and get directions.

Public locations are not necessarily designated exchange zones or monitored. Verify suitability, access and opening hours before traveling. Follow any applicable custody agreement.

Two people. One destination. Life meets in the middle.
